# 24 November 2026

Preview only: `/v7-preview/smallmagic/` on the PR 2 branch. Live `/v7/` was not changed. Trees were not rebuilt or moved. Mage stays staffless. Summoner and Puppetmaster stay separate.

## Block 15 — classes

Summoner (staff, grimoire, catch beam, ghost summons, a small heal aura) and Puppetmaster (Black Ant tank, Crow fighter, chest nesting, string recall) are separate kits under Mage. Porcelain healer was not built.

## Block 16 — looks

Sharper High textures, living idles, warmer light, soft fill, gentle bloom with a phone cap, and contact shadows. A repeating hex event no longer pays Resonance twice.

## Block 17 — town square

Seeded square between cottage, keep, and abbey: Kenney houses, fountain, stalls, lanterns, notice board, benches, planters, and a cobble bow. Folk sit, browse, and gather at dusk. Phone uses lighter copies of the distant houses.

## Block 18 — leftovers and fun

Pet battles use the over-the-shoulder camera. Moss boar, an original puddle slime, and a goblin-cub joined the meadow. Sprint feet plant instead of skating. Rare catches and hex clears get a short reward. The Command Board can point at the square or a hex. The first two minutes suggest a next step.

## Block 19 — AR floor

WebXR hit-test places a reticle when the device supports it. Otherwise the floor is an eye-height estimate. iPhone keeps tap-to-anchor, slow drift correction, and Recenter. A gateway beast sniffs and can be fought or befriended. Spells leave a short floor mark.

## Block 20 — WebXR camera

An immersive session renders from the viewer pose and restores the meadow camera on exit. Select fires the equipped action along the gaze. Beasts, the pet, the gateway, and a small floating HUD sit at real-world size. Settings names which XR path is active.

## Block 21 — three live kits and a spell wheel

Warden (ward, bubble, snare, calm), Ranger (draw, volley, trap, hawk mark), and Alchemist (heal, acid, smoke, rain/fire brew) are live cards on the pad, in AR, and in the raid. Squeeze or a long select opens a gaze wheel. Herbs start at 3 on a new save.

## Block 22 — stability and phone cost

A headless phone pass walks the nine classes, the raid, a pet bout, Long Lake, Home Hub, the Command Board, and the square. Bad saves migrate instead of crashing. Phone ground and distant houses are cheaper, and the arms model loads when a class needs it.

## Block 23 — hit feel and sound

Heavy hits freeze the world for a short beat, shake the camera by how far the hit overshoots, and pop a damage number. Beasts flinch. Weak spots sparkle. Kenney CC0 clips cover casts, steps, rarities, and clicks. Master, music, and effects sliders default moderate. Warden snare slows the brute. Alchemist smoke makes it lose lock. A phone vibrates on a bite, a heavy hit, and a catch.

## Block 24 — townsfolk and beds

Folk keep a seeded day: sweep, water, read the notice, talk at the well, and go home with a lantern. Rain gets an umbrella or a duck under the eaves. A big spell nearby makes them step back. A rare visitor draws a small crowd. The rare-NPC tell stays a faint foot ring. Ambient beds are CC0 field recordings. The gun clip is softened. Hit-stop no longer freezes your own steps.

## Block 25 — hex and raid spectacle

A live hex event raises a colored curtain you can see from the square. Runes pulse when you step in. The brute’s wind-up fills a ground ring. The 50% break tips the camera, sends a shockwave, and drops rubble scaled by the overshoot. The win holds a beat, then shows the myth-horn card. Frost spreads on wet ground. Fire leaves a scorch. Rain turns the tornado into a waterspout. Growth plants flowers. Phone counts stay lower.

## Block 26 — fewer curtains, better marks

Only one or two hexes, chosen from the clock’s four-hour band, hold an event. Clearing one waits for the next band instead of lighting again. Growth flowers are upright crossed stems that pop and sway. Scorch is an irregular soft blot with ember flecks.

- Porcelain healer is still later. Hawk rarity is the day’s seeded roll, not a stored spawn tier.
